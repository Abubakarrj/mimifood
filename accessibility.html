Accessibility

Updated October 2026.

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to browse our coffee, order and manage a subscription on this website, whatever device or assistive technology they use. We work towards the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Screen readers: buttons, links, forms and photos have labels and descriptions, and pages use proper headings.
  • Keyboard: everything can be reached with the keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
  • Reading: text and buttons meet contrast guidelines, and the page can be zoomed without breaking.
  • Motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, animations are turned off.
  • Forms: mistakes are explained in words, not by color alone.

Reading aids

The blue button in the bottom-left corner of every page opens a few options: larger text, higher contrast, underlined links, an easy-read font and stopping animations. Your choices stay on as you move around the site, on this device.

Known limits

Some things come from other companies, such as card payments and maps, and follow their own accessibility standards. We keep checking our own pages as we change them, and some may still fall short.

Tell us

If anything on this website is hard to use, or you'd like information in another format, email hello@mimifood.com. We usually reply within a day, and can help you place your order another way in the meantime.
