Privacy & Cookie Policy

Updated October 2026.

What we collect

  • Orders: your name, email, phone, delivery address, what you ordered and any gift message, so we can make, ship or deliver it and contact you about it.
  • Accounts and subscriptions: your name, email, mobile number if you add one, and your subscription choices and delivery address. If you add a passkey, we keep only its public key; your device keeps the rest.
  • Payments: card details go straight to Square. We never see or store your card number.
  • Newsletter: your email, once you confirm it.
  • Follow-up emails: if you start a checkout or place an order, we may email you about it: a reminder if a checkout wasn't finished, or news of new products if it's been a while. Each email has a link to stop them.
  • Gift cards: the name, email and (if given) mobile number of the person a gift card is for, to send it to them, and who it's from.
  • Wholesale: the business and contact details you send us, and the mobile number on the account for login codes.
  • Ambassadors: your name, email, city, social accounts, follower range and what you tell us, to review your application and run the program.
  • Job applications: your name, email, phone if you give it, links to your work, your CV and what you tell us, to review your application. An assistant reads each one against the role and suggests a grade with its reasons; a person on our team makes every decision. Only our team sees them. Ask us and we'll delete your application and CV.
  • Questions on the FAQ page: what you type, to answer it. We keep the words of a question (not who asked it) for 180 days, to see what people want to know and write about it.
  • Problems with an order: what you tell us and the photos you send when something arrives damaged, wrong or missing, so our team can replace or refund it. Only our team sees the photos; they're kept with your order.
  • Messages to us: what you write in the chat, the contact form, by email or by text, with your name and email if you give them, so we can reply. We also sort messages by what they're about (an order, a question, a problem) to answer the most urgent first.
  • Your visits, when signed in: if you've allowed analytics, which pages you view, for how long, and what you add to your cart, kept for 90 days, so we can help you better. Nothing is kept when you're signed out or have said no to analytics.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the shop, and only what each one needs:

  • Square: payments, subscriptions, invoices and our point of sale.
  • Uber Direct: your name, phone and address when a courier delivers your order.
  • Shippo and the carrier: your name and address on shipping labels.
  • Twilio: your mobile number, to text a wholesale login code.
  • Meta (Instagram): an ambassador applicant's Instagram username, to look up their public follower and post numbers.
  • AnyAPI: a wholesale inquiry's business name, city, and website or Instagram, and an ambassador applicant's Instagram username, to look up the business's public Google Maps listing, website and Instagram numbers for our team. When our team looks up a customer, their email address, to see their public work profile (job title, company and public profile links). We keep that for one year and delete it with your account. Ask us and we'll show it to you or delete it. For a return by mail, the map point of your address (not your name or street), to find the nearest drop-off places.
  • Resend: sends our emails (order confirmations, login codes, replies to your messages, the newsletter and follow-up emails) and receives your email replies to us.
  • OpenStreetMap: checks your address for courier delivery.
  • Anthropic: answers the questions you ask on the FAQ page, sorts messages to us by what they're about, and reads job applications (your CV and what you wrote, not your name, email or phone) to suggest a grade for our team.
  • Google Analytics: only if you turn on analytics cookies. How you use the site and what you buy, without your name, email or address.

We don't sell your information. It's only used for advertising if you turn on marketing cookies.

How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for as long as tax law requires. Account details are kept until you delete your account. Unconfirmed newsletter signups are deleted after 7 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account from your account page. Subscriptions are cancelled, your login and newsletter signup are removed, and past orders keep only what tax records need, without your name, email or address.
  • Stop the newsletter and follow-up emails with the unsubscribe link in any of them, or write to us. Order and delivery emails still arrive.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or a correction, by emailing .
