What Is True Specialty®?

A higher standard. A deeper experience.

Great coffee and tea are more than a score, a roast profile, or a place of origin. They are the result of countless intentional decisions, from the hands that cultivate them to the moment they reach your cup.

At Mimi Food, we believe exceptional coffee and tea deserve to be recognized, understood, and experienced.

That belief led us to True Specialty®, our philosophy of uncompromising quality and a commitment to making extraordinary coffee and tea more accessible.

True Specialty® represents more than what we taste. It represents everything that goes into creating something remarkable.

The Philosophy

Beyond the Five Senses

We call this the sixth sense.

There are experiences that words cannot fully describe. A familiar aroma. An unexpected sweetness. A moment of clarity. The feeling that something is simply right.

It is the intuition that allows us to recognize something extraordinary, even before we can explain why.

Mimi Food was founded on the belief that a cup of coffee or tea can awaken this sense. Not through complexity for its own sake, but through intention, precision, and an uncompromising pursuit of quality.

Our purpose is to make that experience possible in every cup.

Why Light Roast?

To Reveal, Not Conceal.

Because the best coffee doesn’t need to be transformed. It needs to be understood.

Every coffee carries the marks of where and how it was grown: its soil, altitude and climate, its variety, and how it was processed. We believe these distinctions deserve to be experienced.

Light roasting allows us to celebrate the individuality of each coffee, revealing delicate aromatics, vibrant acidity, natural sweetness, and layered complexity.

Rather than imposing a uniform roast character, we approach every coffee with the intention of expressing what makes it extraordinary.
